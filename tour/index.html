Getting started

1Choose your device

Everyone

The first time you open the site, it asks which device you are using. This makes sure the site fits your screen.

  1. Open the website. A window asks Which device are you using?
  2. Press Auto choose the right device, or tap Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Linux, iPad, iPhone or Android.
  3. Press Done. If your pick does not match your device, you will see an error. Pick the device you are really on.
  4. Change it any time with the Device config button at the bottom of the page.

2Log in

Students and teachers

Log in so the site remembers your coins, your progress and your friends.

  1. Press Login at the top of the page.
  2. Choose Student or Teacher.
  3. Students: pick your period, search for your name, then type your 10 digit password.
  4. Teachers: pick your name, then enter the site password and the classroom confirmation code.
  5. Press Log in. You stay logged in on that device until you log out.
Keep your password private. Never share it with another student.

3Home

Everyone

The Home page says hello and has quick buttons to start practicing or find your period.

  1. Press Start practicing to jump into science questions.
  2. Press Find my period to see your class schedule.
  3. Use the tabs at the top to move around. Use the arrows to slide the tabs left and right.
Learn and earn

4Practice and Study

Students

Answer science questions by unit. Right answers earn V Coins.

  1. Pick a Unit and a Level: Easy, Mid or Hard.
  2. Choose a mode: Stage Practice, Custom Quiz, Study and Review or Challenge me as I go.
  3. Read the question and tap your answer.
  4. Bonus questions are worth 5 to 10 coins. Keep a streak going for extra rewards.
  5. Press Assignments to see work from your teacher.

5Currency

Students

Your V Coins and the class EXP bar, all in one place.

  1. Class EXP is shared by the whole class. Your teacher hands it out, up to 500.
  2. My V Coins are yours. Earn them by practicing and by finishing lessons.
  3. Watch the progress bar for your next goal and milestone rewards.
  4. Your first login gives you a free welcome gift of 100 coins.

6Game Room

Students

Spend V Coins on play time and play games with the class.

  1. Check your coins at the top.
  2. Pick a game, for example Block Dash, Swing Man, Memory Lab or Bubble Pop.
  3. Choose how much play time to buy with your coins.
  4. Press Play. The clock only runs while you are playing.

7Calculating

Everyone

Numbers about how you and your class are doing.

  1. See your time on the website, your V Coins, class XP and questions right.
  2. Scroll down for My last 7 days and how every period compares.

8Mr.V Enterprise

Students

Extra perks for your account, like bonus coins and more ways to make your profile yours.

  1. You start on the Free plan.
  2. Compare Free, Starter Enterprise and Enterprise+ to see the perks.
  3. Ask Mr. V about Mr.V Enterprise. The owner switches a plan on for your account.
Class life

9Dates

Everyone

The school calendar from September 2026 to June 2027.

  1. Use the arrows to change month.
  2. Click a date to read about it.
  3. Check Coming up for tests, exams and events.
  4. Fun Friday requests open on Fridays.

10Periods

Everyone

The school day runs 8:16 AM to 3:12 PM. Periods are about 25 to 30 minutes.

  1. The top bar shows where the school day is right now.
  2. Look at each day to see the start and end time of every period.
  3. On Tuesdays there are 10 blocks because of KORT.

11Join Classroom

Students

Connect your account to your period.

  1. Choose your period.
  2. Type the classroom confirmation code from your teacher.
  3. Press Join classroom.

12Friends and chat

Students

Add friends from your class and chat. Teachers can turn making friends on or off for each period.

  1. Find someone at the bottom of the page and send a request.
  2. Accept requests in Friend requests.
  3. Open My friends to chat. You can send pictures and reactions.

13Join Game

Students

Play live games with the class, like a quiz on the big screen.

  1. Your teacher shows a 6 digit game code.
  2. Open Join Game and type the code.
  3. Your profile joins the game. Look at the big screen and answer fast.
Make it yours

14My Profile

Everyone

Make it yours with a school friendly picture, color and badge.

  1. Enter your birthday to unlock the picture and profile settings.
  2. Upload a picture or choose a profile color and badge emoji.
  3. Write a short status.
  4. Press Save profile.

15Settings

Everyone

Turn things on or off so the site feels right for you. These are saved for your account on this device.

  1. Pop-up notifications, speed and big pop-ups.
  2. Sound effects and volume.
  3. Show the tour when you log in, high contrast and an easy read font.

16Notifications

Everyone

Everything you have been told, newest first.

  1. Press the bell at the top to jump here. A red number shows what is new.
  2. Press Delete on one, or Clear all.
For teachers

17Teacher Tools

Teachers

One window with everything a teacher needs to run the class.

  1. XP and Coins: give class XP and set coin payouts.
  2. Notify: send a small or big pop-up.
  3. Control online, Online and log outs: see who is on and log students out.
  4. Create a game: start a live game with a code.
  5. Suspend or ban and Lock tabs: keep the class on track.
  6. Students, Friends and chat, Dates, Fun Friday, Music, Site color and User info.

18Presentations

Teachers make, students watch

Teachers build slide shows with buttons, checklists, pictures and more.

  1. Teachers press New presentation.
  2. Add text, pictures or GIFs, shapes, drawings, sticky notes, buttons, due lists and audio.
  3. Press Save, then Present.
  4. Students open the Presentations tab to see the slide shows.
On the go

19On your phone

Everyone

Everything above works on a phone or tablet. The tabs slide sideways at the top.

  1. Swipe the tab bar to see more pages.
  2. Use Device config to pick iPhone, iPad or Android.
